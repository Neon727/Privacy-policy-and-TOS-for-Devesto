# Devesto Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 9/16/26

This Privacy Policy explains what data Devesto ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects when you use it, how that data is used, and your rights regarding it.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Discord IDs:** Your Discord user ID and the IDs of servers ("guilds") you use Devesto in. These are used to associate settings and data with the correct user/server.
- **Message content (limited, feature-specific):** Devesto only reads message content where a specific feature requires it:
  - AutoMod (filtering banned words, spam, invite links)
  - Leveling (awarding XP based on chat activity)
  - Markov chat-learning (`/mimic`, opt-in per server)
  - Tags, sticky messages, and similar stored-text features
  - We do not log, store, or review message content outside of what a specific active feature requires.
- **Feature-generated data:** Depending on which features a server enables, this may include: economy balances and inventory, pet/adventure stats, leveling XP, tickets/appeals/suggestions you submit, birthdays, AFK statuses, invite tracking, and similar.
- **Server configuration data:** Channel IDs, role IDs, and settings that staff configure via `/dashboard` (e.g. welcome channel, AutoMod rules).

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not read direct messages, and we do not read messages in channels/servers Devesto is not added to.
- We do not collect payment information (any Patreon support is handled entirely by Patreon, not Devesto).
- We do not knowingly collect data from users under Discord's minimum age requirement beyond what Discord itself provides to bots.

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used solely to operate Devesto's features (moderation, economy, leveling, tickets, etc.) within the servers where it is enabled. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties or advertisers.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Certain optional features call external APIs to function, which may receive limited data (typically a search query or public message content, never your Discord ID):

- YouTube Data API (upload notifications)
- OpenTDB (trivia questions)
- Urban Dictionary API (slang lookups)
- OCR.space (image text scanning for scam detection)
- Google Translate (translation features)

These services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review if concerned.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained for as long as a feature is in use. If Devesto is removed from a server, guild-specific configuration data may be deleted automatically or during periodic cleanup. You may request deletion of your personal data (economy balance, leveling stats, etc.) at any time by contacting the bot owner (see Section 8).

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no system is 100% secure. Data is stored on private infrastructure not publicly accessible.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Devesto is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own Terms of Service, which requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect additional data from children beyond what Discord provides to any bot in a server.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Devesto's features change. Continued use of Devesto after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Material changes will be reflected in commands or announcements where reasonably possible.

## 9. Contact

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the official support server or via the contact method listed on the Devesto website.
