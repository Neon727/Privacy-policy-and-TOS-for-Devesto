What Devesto Collects
• Your Discord user ID and the IDs of servers you use Devesto in
• Message content only where a feature requires it (AutoMod filtering, leveling XP, tags, markov chat learning, sticky messages, etc.) - never sold, never shared with third parties
• Feature-specific data you generate: economy balances, pet stats, tickets/appeals/suggestions you submit, birthdays, AFK statuses, and similar  What Devesto Does NOT Do
• Devesto does not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties
• Devesto does not read DMs or messages in channels it isn't added to 
• Devesto does not collect data from servers you leave (member-specific data is periodically cleaned up)  Your Rights 
• You can request your data be deleted at any time by contacting the bot owner 
• Declining this policy means Devesto's commands won't respond to you, but passive server-protection features (AutoMod, security scanning, logging) still apply server-wide regardless, since those protect the whole server rather than being user-invoked
